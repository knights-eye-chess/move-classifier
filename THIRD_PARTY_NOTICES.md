# Third-party notices

First-party grants do not replace third-party licenses. See
[the installed production inventory](licenses/third-party/INVENTORY.json) for
exact versions, declared licenses, source repositories and copied upstream notice
paths. Preserve the upstream copyright/license notices when redistributing a
covered dependency. Sources and embedded notices in vendor archives remain intact.
An empty notice list means the installed package did not supply a notice file;
consult its declared upstream terms before public redistribution.
